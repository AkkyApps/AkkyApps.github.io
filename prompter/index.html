Questions

Before you start.

Which devices are supported?

Prompter is made for iPhone and iPad. It is also available on compatible Apple silicon Macs as an app designed for iPad.

Is there a subscription?

No. Prompter is a paid app with a one-time purchase and no subscription.

Can I use teleprompter glass?

Yes. Mirror mode flips the full presentation view for common beam-splitter teleprompter hardware.

Can scripts sync between devices?

Yes. You can enable iCloud sync to keep your script library available across your Apple devices.
